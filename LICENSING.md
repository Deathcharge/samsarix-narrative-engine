## Why MPL-2.0

MPL-2.0 is a file-level copyleft license. In practical terms, it preserves access to this project's
covered source files and modifications when they are distributed, while allowing those files to be
combined with a larger open-source or proprietary application. It also includes contributor patent
grants, preserves license and copyright notices, and does not grant rights to Samsarix trademarks.

This balance fits a reusable Python SDK better than either a permissive license that allows closed
forked modifications or a broad copyleft license that can complicate adoption inside larger products.
The license text is used unchanged; this repository does not create another custom license variant.

## What redistribution requires

Read the full license before relying on this summary. Generally, a distributor must:

- keep MPL-covered source files and modifications available under MPL-2.0;
- tell recipients where they can obtain that covered source and the license;
- preserve applicable copyright, patent, warranty, and license notices; and
- avoid imposing terms that restrict recipients' MPL rights in the covered source.

Files in a larger work that do not contain MPL-covered code can remain under other terms. Internal use
without external distribution does not trigger the MPL distribution obligations. Provider SDKs and
generated content remain subject to their own licenses, terms, and rights.
