# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation in this project respectful,
welcoming, and free from harassment. Disagreement about ideas is welcome; attacks on people are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and honest.
- Welcome people of different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them.
- Protect private information shared during a report or review.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sustained disruption, unwanted sexual attention, and
publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where a
person is acting as a representative of the project. It does not claim governance over a Discord
server, event, or other community space that the repository does not operate.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use the repository owner's private GitHub contact or private vulnerability-reporting channel. Do not
post personal information or sensitive evidence in a public issue. A dedicated conduct contact is not
yet configured and remains an owner-controlled release gate in `docs/PRODUCTIZATION.md`.

Maintainers may remove content, warn or temporarily restrict participants, or permanently exclude a
participant when needed to protect the community. Reports should be handled privately, consistently,
and with attention to conflicts of interest. This document does not promise a response time or appeal
body that the current project cannot staff.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
