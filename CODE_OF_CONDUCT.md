# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for all members of the Helix Narrative Engine community. We value the contributions of every individual and believe that diverse perspectives strengthen our project.

---

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

**Be Respectful**: Treat all community members with respect and dignity, regardless of their background, identity, or experience level.

**Be Inclusive**: Welcome and support people of all backgrounds and identities. Make efforts to include those whose voices may be underrepresented.

**Be Professional**: Maintain professional communication in all interactions. Disagreements are natural, but handle them constructively.

**Be Collaborative**: Work together to solve problems and improve the project. Share knowledge and help others learn.

**Be Honest**: Be truthful in your communications and acknowledge mistakes when they occur.

**Be Considerate**: Consider the impact of your words and actions on others. Be mindful of different time zones and communication preferences.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

**Harassment**: Any form of harassment, including but not limited to:
- Offensive comments related to gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, mental illness, neuro(a)typicality, physical appearance, body size, age, race, ethnicity, caste, nationality, or religion
- Unwelcome comments regarding a person's lifestyle choices and practices
- Deliberate misgendering or use of "dead" or rejected names
- Gratuitous or off-topic sexual images or behavior
- Physical contact and simulated physical contact without consent or after a request to stop
- Threats of violence
- Incitement of violence towards any individual or group
- Sustained disruption of community spaces
- Stalking or following
- Doxxing (sharing private information without consent)

**Discrimination**: Any form of discrimination based on protected characteristics or personal attributes.

**Abuse**: Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory language directed at individuals or groups.

**Spam**: Posting irrelevant or promotional content repeatedly.

**Violation of Privacy**: Sharing others' private information without consent.

**Illegal Activity**: Any illegal activity or content that promotes illegal activity.

---

## Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers:

**Email**: [contact email to be provided]  
**GitHub**: Report via GitHub Issues (private)  
**Discord**: Direct message to moderators  

When reporting, please include:

- Description of the incident
- Names of individuals involved
- Context and any relevant links
- Any evidence (screenshots, logs, etc.)
- Your contact information

All reports will be handled confidentially and investigated promptly.

---

## Investigation and Enforcement

The project maintainers will:

1. **Acknowledge** your report within 48 hours
2. **Investigate** the incident thoroughly and fairly
3. **Communicate** findings to all parties involved
4. **Take Action** as appropriate, which may include:
   - Warning the individual
   - Temporary suspension from community spaces
   - Permanent removal from the project
   - Reporting to appropriate authorities (if necessary)

---

## Appeal Process

If you believe a decision was made in error, you may appeal by:

1. Submitting a written appeal within 30 days of the decision
2. Providing new evidence or context
3. Requesting review by a neutral third party

Appeals will be reviewed by project maintainers not involved in the original decision.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub repositories and issues
- Pull request discussions
- Discord server and other chat platforms
- Project events and conferences
- Official project communications
- Any space representing the project or community

---

## Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

1. **First Offense**: Warning and request to cease behavior
2. **Second Offense**: Temporary suspension (7-30 days)
3. **Third Offense**: Permanent removal from community
4. **Severe Violations**: Immediate permanent removal

---

## Accessibility and Inclusion

We are committed to making our community accessible and inclusive:

- Provide accommodations for people with disabilities
- Use inclusive language and avoid jargon when possible
- Be mindful of different communication styles and preferences
- Welcome contributions from people of all experience levels
- Provide mentorship and support for newcomers
